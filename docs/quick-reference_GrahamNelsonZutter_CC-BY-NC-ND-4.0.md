# EA & LT 4FMs Codebook v5.0 - Quick Reference Guide

## Getting Started

### What is this codebook?
A comprehensive framework for analyzing human experiences through the lens of **Four Fundamental Motivations (4FMs)** from Existential Analysis and Logotherapy, based on the work of **Alfried Längle**.

### Who should use it?
- **Researchers**: For qualitative coding of interview/therapy transcripts
- **Clinicians**: For assessment and treatment planning
- **Students**: For learning existential-phenomenological psychology
- **Trainers**: For teaching EA/LT concepts

---

## The Four Fundamental Motivations at a Glance

| FM | Theme | Question | Need | Positive | Negative | Pathology |
|----|-------|----------|------|----------|----------|-----------|
| **FM1** | **World/Reality** | Can I be? | Support, Space, Protection | Trust, Confidence | Fear, Anxiety | Phobic/Anxiety disorders |
| **FM2** | **Life/Value** | Do I like being? | Relationship, Closeness, Value | Joy, Harmony | Depression, Emptiness | Affective disorders |
| **FM3** | **Self/Justice** | May I be myself? | Respect, Appreciation | Self-esteem, Authenticity | Shame, Loneliness | Histrionic/Borderline |
| **FM4** | **Meaning/Future** | What for? | Purpose, Field of Activity | Fulfillment, Dedication | Despair, Addiction | Addiction, Suicidality |

## Quick Coding Guide

### Step 1: Identify the Primary FM
Ask: What existential domain is being addressed?
- **FM1**: Reality, facts, safety, space, protection → "Can I be here?"
- **FM2**: Emotions, relationships, value, closeness → "Is it good to be alive?"
- **FM3**: Identity, justice, respect, authenticity → "May I be myself?"  
- **FM4**: Meaning, purpose, contribution, future → "What is it good for?"

### Step 2: Determine Need Status
- ✅ **Need Met**: Positive outcomes present (trust, joy, authenticity, fulfillment)
- ❌ **Need Unmet**: Negative outcomes present (fear, depression, shame, despair)
- ⚠️ **Blocked**: Coping reactions evident (avoidance, regression, distancing, provisional attitude)

### Step 3: Identify Sub-Theme (if applicable)
Use specific code IDs for detailed analysis:
- **Example**: FM2.ST2 = "Relationship & Closeness"
- **Example**: FM4.ST1 = "Answering the Situation's Call"

### Step 4: Note Coping Reactions (if present)
When FMs are blocked, look for four types:
1. **Basic Reaction**: Primary defense (avoidance, regression, distancing, provisional)
2. **Activism**: Overactivity (compulsive, overprotective, stubborn, fanatical)
3. **Aggression**: Hostile response (hate, rage, anger, cynicism)
4. **Freezing**: Withdrawal (denial, resignation, dissociation, apathy)

## Key Concepts to Remember

### The Noetic Framework
Each FM involves:
- **Content**: What is sought (TRUE, ATTRACTIVE, RIGHT, MEANING)
- **Motivation**: Type of will (to be, to value, to right, to meaning)
- **Mode**: How it's experienced (can, like, may, should)

### The Epistemological Chain
Knowledge progresses through FMs:
1. FM1: **Know** → Facts
2. FM2: **Understand** → Coherence  
3. FM3: **Recognize** → Responsibility
4. FM4: **Act** → Commitment

### Hierarchical Integration
- FMs build on each other: FM1 → FM2 → FM3 → FM4
- Earlier blockages prevent later fulfillment
- All four needed for complete existential fulfillment

### The Three-Step Process (PEA)
Finding meaning involves:
1. **Recognizing**: What does situation offer/demand?
2. **Harmonizing**: Does it fit with my values/abilities?
3. **Inner Consent**: Can I give myself to this?

## Common Coding Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Anxiety about job security
- **Primary FM**: FM1 (threat to existence/space)
- **Need**: Security, protection
- **If met**: Confidence, trust
- **If unmet**: Fear, anxiety
- **Possible coping**: Avoidance or compulsive overwork

### Scenario 2: Feeling disconnected from others
- **Primary FM**: FM2 (lack of relationship/value)
- **Need**: Closeness, emotional connection
- **If met**: Sense of depth, joy
- **If unmet**: Emptiness, depression
- **Possible coping**: Regression or resignation

### Scenario 3: Shame about identity
- **Primary FM**: FM3 (not being seen/accepted as oneself)
- **Need**: Respect, appreciation for who one is
- **If met**: Authenticity, self-esteem
- **If unmet**: Shame, need to hide
- **Possible coping**: Distancing or dissociation

### Scenario 4: Life feels pointless
- **Primary FM**: FM4 (lack of meaning/purpose)
- **Need**: Goal, field of activity, purpose
- **If met**: Fulfillment, dedication
- **If unmet**: Emptiness, despair
- **Possible coping**: Provisional attitude or addiction

## Disambiguation Rules

### When multiple FMs seem relevant:

**FM1 vs. FM2**
- FM1: Physical/factual concern → "Can I survive/exist?"
- FM2: Emotional/relational concern → "Is life worth living?"

**FM2 vs. FM3**  
- FM2: General relationship/value → "Do I feel valued?"
- FM3: Being seen as unique self → "Am I respected for who I am?"

**FM3 vs. FM4**
- FM3: Right to be oneself → "May I be this way?"
- FM4: Contribution/purpose → "What can I give/do?"

**Rule of thumb**: Use the **existential question** that best matches the core concern.

## Inclusion/Exclusion Cheat Sheet

### ✅ Include in FM1
- Reality testing, facts, conditions
- Physical safety, space, protection
- Existential security/insecurity
- Basic trust in world

### ❌ Exclude from FM1
- Emotional value without factual concern → FM2
- Identity/respect issues → FM3
- Purpose/meaning issues → FM4

### ✅ Include in FM2
- Feeling, emotion, being moved
- Relationships, closeness, intimacy
- Joy in living, value of life
- Time and presence

### ❌ Exclude from FM2
- Physical safety alone → FM1
- Recognition of unique self → FM3
- Purpose/contribution → FM4

### ✅ Include in FM3
- Being seen, attended to uniquely
- Justice, fair treatment, respect
- Self-worth, authenticity
- Right to be oneself

### ❌ Exclude from FM3
- General relationship → FM2
- Physical space/existence → FM1
- Meaningful contribution → FM4

### ✅ Include in FM4
- Purpose, meaning, calling
- Future orientation, goals
- Contribution, self-transcendence
- Commitment to valued possibilities

### ❌ Exclude from FM4
- Identity without future orientation → FM3
- Value without commitment → FM2
- Safety/structure alone → FM1

## Assessment Questions Reference

Use these to explore each FM in interviews or clinical work:

**FM1 Questions:**
- Can I be here?
- Is there space for me?
- Do I feel protected and supported?
- Can I trust in my existence?

**FM2 Questions:**
- Do I like being here?
- Is it good that I am alive?
- Do I feel valued and appreciated?
- Can I experience the depth and value of life?

**FM3 Questions:**
- May I be as I am?
- Am I treated with respect and justice?
- Do I have self-worth?
- Can I be authentic?

**FM4 Questions:**
- What is it good for that I am here?
- Do I have a purpose?
- Can I contribute something meaningful?
- What does the situation ask of me?

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Mistake 1**: Coding everything as FM4
- Reality check: Not everything is about meaning/purpose
- Each FM has distinct territory

❌ **Mistake 2**: Ignoring coping reactions
- Defensive patterns reveal blocked FMs
- Code the coping type for complete picture

❌ **Mistake 3**: Using only primary FMs
- Sub-codes provide specificity
- Better reliability with detailed coding

❌ **Mistake 4**: Confusing need status
- Met ≠ Blocked ≠ Unmet
- Be precise about which outcome is present

❌ **Mistake 5**: Adding non-source content
- Stay grounded in Längle's framework
- Don't import concepts from other theories
